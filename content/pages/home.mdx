Kiterae Vertical is written for commercial teams evaluating vertical gardening as infrastructure, not decor. The market is full of hardware, tower farms, container farms, consumer devices, and inspiration galleries. The harder question is operational: who seeds, monitors, harvests, cleans, services, reports, and owns the outcome after installation day?

For commercial real estate teams, the opportunity is a living asset that can help differentiate space, support wellness and ESG goals, and give tenants something tangible to experience. For hospitality operators, the promise is tighter control over specialty herbs, microgreens, edible flowers, and other high-value ingredients where freshness and availability shape the guest experience.

The white space is managed service. A vertical garden can create value only if the system stays productive, attractive, and easy to explain. That requires a coordinated stack: hardware, controlled-environment software, crop planning, field service, harvesting, maintenance, and clear reporting. Buying equipment is not the same as buying that operating model.

This field guide helps buyers separate the visible farm from the work that makes it dependable. Start with the roadmap, move through cost and service design, then use the CRE and hospitality guides to frame the business case for your own space.
